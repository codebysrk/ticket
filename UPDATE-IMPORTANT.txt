Automatic update behavior is enabled.

The installed PWA:
1. Checks for a newer service worker when it opens.
2. Uses network-first for index.html.
3. Automatically activates a new service worker.
4. Reloads once when the new worker takes control.

IMPORTANT FOR HOSTING:
Serve sw.js with Cache-Control: no-cache, no-store, or must-revalidate.
Serve index.html with Cache-Control: no-cache or a short max-age.

For Netlify, add a _headers file with:
/sw.js
  Cache-Control: no-cache, no-store, must-revalidate

/index.html
  Cache-Control: no-cache, must-revalidate
